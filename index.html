A FEW THINGS TO REMEMBER

Care notes.

Good plant care is mostly paying attention.

✳

START HERE

Get to know
your light.

Most houseplants are happiest near a bright window, just out of the harshest afternoon sun.

01

Check the soil first

Touch the top inch. If it still feels damp, give it another day or two.

02

Give it a proper drink

Water slowly until a little drains from the bottom, then empty the saucer.

03

Look for the little signs

Drooping leaves, dry edges, and pale patches can tell you when something needs to change.
